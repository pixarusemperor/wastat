Details:
Block Contact

 Blocks a specific contact.

Parameters:
- contactPhoneNumber (string, required): The JID (Jabber ID) of the contact in E.164 format (international phone number) e.g., 1234567890.

#[tokio::main]

        .send()

    Ok(())
